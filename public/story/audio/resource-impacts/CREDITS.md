## Runtime mapping

- Plants, berries, blossoms, moss, reeds, and mushrooms: foliage rustle.
- Trees, bark, amber-bearing wood, and palmwood: wood strikes and snaps.
- Fieldstone, coal, sand deposits, and ordinary rock: rock strike and collapse.
- Iron and silver ore: metallic strikes layered with rock collapse on depletion.
- Gravebone: dedicated bone rattle/crack.
- Obsidian, opal, soul salt, violet cores, charged ore, sunstone, and prisms: glass/crystal impacts.
- Glacial Crystal and Everfrost: crystal sounds pitched higher for ice.
- Basalt and Emberdeep volcanic deposits: rock sounds pitched lower and heavier.
