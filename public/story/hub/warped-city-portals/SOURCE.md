# Warped City storefront compositions

Door, sign, terminal, antenna, and control-box artwork by Ansimuz, downloaded from OpenGameArt and released under CC0 1.0. K.O.R.E. combines the original transparent parts into eleven destination-specific storefront silhouettes without redrawing them.
